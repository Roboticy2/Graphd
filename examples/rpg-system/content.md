# Quests
Reka is the "main" character. She is looking for her lost friends, who were kidnapped in front of her around one week ago. 

The goal of this demo, for the party, is to formulate a plan to find them. 
 - Geno has some knowledge of motion in the criminal underground. He knows of a ring out in the desert, but has to roll a die to admit it, and won't admit it in front of Freya. If he fails, he gains a thought which can help him.
 - REDACTED can perform magic on a sentimental portrait of the two victims. He remembers he has this magic inside the magic cave in the upper square. In the future, I want him to have spell slots + limited uses, but that's out of scope for this demo.
 - Freya can get information out of Ace by threatening to arrest him. Ace gives her a note "don't go into the caves at night." The message is visible when inspected in the inventory. She has the second goal of finding out who REDACTED is. Playing chess with him, talking to Ace, and following him into the cave will all give more information here.
 - Reka has multiple dice rolls to try to get a hold of herself. If she succeeds, she gives relevant information, otherwise, she panics, threatening who she's talking to or saying something unintelligible. Other characters all relay findings to her after finding them. The demo is complete when all findings are relayed to Reka.

# Experience
Progressing dialogue yields experience the first time a character passes through that dialogue. The dialogue screen does not show experience, but it should show a message when the character levels up.

# Bonus stuff
Each playable character comes with their own equipment- 3 or 4 default pieces with maybe some optional replacements -plus some non-equipment items. These serve to better define their character for players curious enough to explore the inventory screen.

# Design Guidelines
While this demo is inspired by Disco Elysium, I'm not ZAUM. I shouldn't try to write like them. This is a technical demonstration, also. Spending too long on writing would take away from other parts of the project. Content should be short and functional, and nothing not mentioned in this document should be included without very good reason.
